# Inventory and Sales Management System

I made this project while learning SQL. I wanted to understand how a small shop actually keeps track of its stock and sales, so I built the whole thing in MySQL.

## What it does:

- Keeps supplier, product and customer details
- Records every sale with items and quantity
- Calculates current stock automatically
- Shows low stock products so we know what to reorder
- Shows which products and categories earn more revenue
- Monthly sales report and a simple sales summary view

I used Joins, GROUP BY, CASE WHEN, Views and Transactions in this project. It helped me understand how data connects in real life, not just in theory.

Skills: SQL, MySQL, Excel, Power BI, Python
